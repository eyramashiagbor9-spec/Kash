🐶 About Dogs

Dogs are domesticated animals that have lived alongside humans for thousands of years. They can be companions, guard dogs, guide dogs and working animals.

Dogs come in many different sizes, shapes, colours and personalities. Some are energetic and playful, while others are calm and protective.
